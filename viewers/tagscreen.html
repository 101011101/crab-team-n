For whoever is holding it

  1. Screen brightness to maximum.
  2. Fullscreen, and keep the screen flat-on to the camera.
  3. Avoid glare — a reflection across the tag stops detection dead. Tilt away from lights.
  4. Hold still. Motion blur hurts more than distance.
  5. Do not zoom the browser. Zoom changes the real size.
