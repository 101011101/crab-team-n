What this map gets wrong The walk went straight down the corridor, so depth ahead of the camera is the weakest thing in the reconstruction. Past x ≈ 4 m the floor fans out into radial streaks and the walls there are extrapolation, not measurement. The recovered path also bows — off its own chord while the heading stays fixed, so the corridor is drawn with a bend it almost certainly does not have. Closed doors sit flush with the wall and are occupied cells like any other: this map cannot see them.
