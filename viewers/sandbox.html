Why a crab decides to move the box

Three goals can be open at once — UNBLOCK, EXPLORE, REACH — and every open goal must have an owner. Ownership is latched: a crab that starts a shove finishes it. A crab holding REACH while the reward is still walled off neither idles nor scouts; it stages at the gap, because the route that will exist once the box moves is the shortest one it has.

The arena is drawn for the chassis, not for a point: every gap is 0.90 m, which leaves a 0.56 m planning band once the 0.17 m margin is taken off each wall. A crab is a 0.20 × 0.15 m rectangle and collides as one. It sees a 30° cone out to 0.80 m, and it steers rather than pivots: the tightest turn is 0.34 m, so a target more than 109° off its nose is reached by backing up. The box moves only when a crab drives into it — touching it side-on does nothing. Source: viewers/sandbox.html, sb_core.js, sb_sim.js.
