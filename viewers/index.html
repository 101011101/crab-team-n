  • Field Sandbox — why a crab moves the boxlive Rectangular crabs, a real hitbox, and a box that moves only when a crab drives into it. Each crab holds one reach field and asks whether a cheaper world exists. Three goals open at once — UNBLOCK, EXPLORE, REACH — every one latched to an owner. Leave the second doorway open and nobody touches the box; seal it and one crab shoves it clear while another waits at the gap. 10/10 runs reach the reward · push derived, not scripted · 0 collisions
  • Swarm Mission Replaylive Two crabs explore, hit a blocked corridor, and derive the collaboration that clears it. Second tab: every waypoint command as it would reach the robot. 4/4 species · 43.2 s · deterministic
  • Two Routes, One Rewardlive Same code, two worlds. With the south corridor open a crab detours; sealed, the solver emits a push instead. Nobody scheduled the race. 61.6 s detour · 33.7 s push
  • Robot 1’s First Roomlive The first 3D world built from an actual robot, not a phone. 49 seconds of QVGA video from one ESP32-CAM. The solved camera trace is drawn beside the wheel dead-reckoning trace, because agreeing twice is the only evidence here. What is assumed is labelled as assumed. 70/125 frames · 0.790 px · 155k points · SfM vs wheels 5.2 cm RMS
  • Corridor Point Cloudlive The 3D world rebuilt from phone video. Compare the solved reconstruction against the depth shell an ESP32-CAM would produce. 124/124 poses · 0.815 px · 14.4 × 4.7 × 3.1 m
  • Arena Editorlive Draw walls, drop a reward and a movable block, then export JSON or ready-to-paste Python straight into the simulator. Checks chassis clearance and whether the reward is reachable without moving anything. opens on the race arena · 0.18 m chassis · undo, import, autosave
  • Corridor Bird's-Eye Maplive The 3D world collapsed to a labelled top-down occupancy map — what the robots would plan on. Its failures are marked, not hidden. width 1.44 m · camera height 1.672 ± 0.050 m · 13 walls
  • Where the Camera Walkedlive A real webcam walk around one printed AprilTag, drawn in the tag's frame. The whole localization chain, proved before a robot exists. pose to <1 mm · ambiguous poses refused
  • AprilTag Screen Targetlive Open it on a laptop and the screen becomes a calibrated tag. It measures the display and prints the true tag size, so the scale is right without a printer. emailable · size read off the screen, not assumed
