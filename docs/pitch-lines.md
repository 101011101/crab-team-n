## Cleaned lines

- A team of super cheap robots that collaborate, explore, and solve problems.
- Crab Team N. A play on Seal Team 6.
- A world from multiple visions.
- Make a world from multiple locations.

## Stated uses

- Training bigger robots. Like deploying the military.
- Search and rescue.
- Exploration and collection.
- Difficult, non-uniform shaped problems.
- Spaces too small for large robots.

## Notes

- "A world from multiple visions" is the strongest line. It states the technical claim and the product claim at once.
- Name settled: Crab Team N. The N carries the scaling claim, so the count never dates the name.
